Who we are

From design
to turnover.

MIMAG Construction and Trading Corporation is an engineering-led contractor covering design, project management, construction execution, maintenance and trading.

Formerly MIMAG Design and Construction, the company was registered in 2016 at the Department of Trade and Industries and later incorporated with the Securities and Exchange Commission in 2021.

MIMAG works with private and public sectors on new and refurbishment projects across civil engineering, electro-mechanical engineering and architecture.

Strategically based in Pampanga, MIMAG describes itself as “close to customer,” mobilizing for small, medium and large works across the regions.
